40% release coverage

The bounded method released 240 of 600 inputs without a wrong release. It rejected every near-origin, high-frequency and missing-sector input.

Method

On each interval, the observed segment must stay farther from the origin than max(endpoint errors) + L × angular gap / 2. The resulting homotopy preserves the received-loop winding under the supplied assumptions.

Measured complex samples → error discs → global derivative bound → origin exclusion
